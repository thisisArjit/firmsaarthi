# Supplier directory separation — 30 October 2026 IST

Supplier staging introduces an explicit, owner-published business profile projection separate from a transporter's private supplier master, ratings, assignments, invoices and settlements. Discovery must never copy private notes, registration plates, driver identities, documents or account identities into a shared listing. Business contact is separately entered and reviewed. A listing is hidden on every edit until republished. Server reads enforce account/membership/business mode; supplier-only users do not gain Transport operations or finance. Existing transporters may explicitly maintain their own listing without converting workspace or changing issued history. No automatic master merge or billing recognition follows from discovery or publication. Contacts already viewed cannot be retracted from a recipient's memory/device; hide blocks future directory reads.

Open supplier forms stay mounted across tabs; a version conflict preserves the form. Unconfirmed request recovery uses a per-account session key and exact retry, not an overwrite or a second record. See SUPPLIER-PRODUCT.md and SUPPLIER-CHECKPOINT.md. Broader demand/public visibility, staff editing, enquiries and sensitive documents require reviewed follow-up workflows.
