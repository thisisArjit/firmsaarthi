[Google OIDC](https://developers.google.com/identity/openid-connect/openid-connect) specifies stable subject identity. [Resend idempotency](https://resend.com/changelog/idempotency-keys) documents request-specific keys and24-hour retention. Decision: bind one mail attempt to its challenge hash, separate signup/reset namespaces and keep account completion idempotent independently of mail delivery.
