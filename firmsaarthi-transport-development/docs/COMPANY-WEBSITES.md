# Public company websites

Settings → Company website is owner-only. Owners choose an available path such as transport.firmsaarthi.com/stta, save a draft, review the exact public template, and explicitly approve publication. No company is published by deployment. No separate DNS or registrar purchase is needed for these paths; customer-owned domains are not connected by this feature.

The initial draft copies only business name, office address and contact number from business settings. Optional route suggestions use that workspace's origin/destination pairs; the owner must select them. Bank, tax, customer, invoice, document, staff and tracking details are excluded. Published data is an independent allowlisted snapshot and does not silently change with business records or unfinished edits. Each page has its own title and description, accessible section navigation and direct phone/email/WhatsApp links. No enquiry delivery service, live availability, verification badge or invented claims are included.

One globally unique slug per workspace, reserved application paths, bounded values, escaped text, strict contact protocols, owner checks, same-origin mutation checks, support-session denial, atomic version checks and audit events protect the flow. Addresses become permanent after first publication, including while unpublished, to avoid broken links and reallocation impersonation. Draft editing, republishing and unpublishing remain available to the owner independently of subscription expiry. Normal authenticated workspace access still follows billing policy. Public reads retrieve only the published JSON, never draft or organization data. Unknown/unpublished addresses return 404.

Tests cover validation, public allowlists and HTML escaping, tenant/role boundaries, uniqueness, stale save/publish conflicts, publication consent, contact requirements, draft isolation, unpublication, auditing and unchanged financial tables. Hosted desktop/phone testing must cover the real editor and anonymous public page, with synthetic staging content only, before production. The real STTA page remains unpublished until its owner approves the details.
