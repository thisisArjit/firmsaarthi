## Trial and pricing

- Public sign-in/signup offer and authenticated banners use the same server policy. Defaults: 10 days, 199900 integer paise/month. No card is collected or automatically charged.
- The policy is initialized once outside migrations when first requested. Existing workspaces get a full rollout trial anchored at that policy initialization, never retroactively expired from their old creation date. Workspaces created afterwards start from their creation timestamp. Each workspace's stored trial dates are immutable: sign-in/out, new members, refresh, redeployment and future policy edits do not restart or extend them.
- The administrator can change trial length, price and optional customer-facing payment-enquiry instructions. Trial changes apply only to newly initialized trials; previous trial dates remain fixed. Prices apply to future manual activations and each confirmation retains the amount and period snapshot. There is no negotiated per-customer price yet; use an exemption for complimentary accounts.

## Administrator and customer controls

- `/subscription`: own workspace plan, expiry, activation request and owner-only payment confirmation history/export. Team members cannot request activation or modify subscription settings. Existing application passwords, login/signup, recovery and role boundaries remain unchanged.
- `/subscription/admin`: customer search (100 results; narrow by business or owner email), current status, activation requests, optional dated/indefinite exemption, reasoned exemption removal, manual monthly activation and global policy configuration. This returns billing identity/access metadata, not other workspaces' consignments, invoices or financial records.
- Platform authority is pinned to `BILLING_ADMIN_USER_ID` in the hosted runtime, using the immutable original Site-owner application user ID already established by the migration script. It is not inferred from an email address, organization name, a client flag, or the ordinary workspace OWNER role. The configured administrator's own workspace is exempt. Missing configuration fails closed for administration; it never makes the first registrant an administrator. This setting was configured as a Sites secret, preserving existing runtime keys.
- Ordinary customers cannot grant exemptions, change dates/prices, read the customer list or activate payments. Exemption changes require the current subscription version and a reason. Internal exemption reasons are excluded from customer/team billing state and record exports; their audit stays in the administrator workspace. Changes apply to the whole team. Listing customers derives uninitialized trial states without creating their subscription rows.
